Our Features

The Campus Equipment Security System offers a range of powerful features to help you register, verify, and protect your devices — all in one secure platform.

Key Features

Everything you need to keep your devices safe and your campus secure.

👤

User Management

Register and manage student, staff, and authorized accounts. Keep your information secure and up to date.

💻

Device Registration

Easily register your laptops and other devices with unique details such as serial numbers and ownership information.

🛡

Ownership Verification

Security personnel can verify devices at campus gates and check if they belong to the person carrying them.

⚠

Lost & Stolen Reporting

Report lost or stolen devices quickly, track their status, and help improve campus security.

🔔

Notifications & Alerts

Get updates about your device status, report progress, and important security notifications.

✓

Secure & Reliable

Your information is protected with secure access controls and organized device management.

💡
