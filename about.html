About Campus Equipment Security System

A digital system designed to help protect, register, verify, and manage laptops and other valuable devices within the university campus.

What Is This System?

The Campus Equipment Security System is designed to improve the way university campuses manage and protect personal and institutional devices.

Instead of depending entirely on manual records, security personnel can use a structured system to identify registered devices and verify their ownership.

Our Main Purpose

The main purpose of this system is to create a safer and more organized environment for students, staff, visitors, and university security personnel.

Device Registration

Register laptops and other devices using important identification and ownership information.

Ownership Verification

Help security personnel verify whether a device belongs to the person carrying it.

Lost & Stolen Reporting

Allow users to report devices that have been lost or stolen and keep a record of the report.

How It Works

01

Create an Account

Students, staff, or authorized users create an account and provide their required information.

02

Register a Device

The user registers a laptop or other device using information such as its serial number.

03

Verify at the Campus

Security personnel can verify the device and compare it with the registered ownership record.

04

Report a Problem

If a device is lost or stolen, the owner can submit a report so the incident can be recorded and followed.

Who Is It For?

Students

Register and manage personal devices used on campus.

University Staff

Register university equipment and maintain device ownership information.

Security Personnel

Verify devices and access the information needed during campus entry and exit checks.

Visitors

Support temporary device registration when required during university events or visits.

Future Vision

This prototype is designed for the University of Rwanda, but the concept can be expanded to other campuses and institutions in the future.

Future development could include stronger device-management capabilities, improved reporting, notifications, and integration with additional security technologies.
